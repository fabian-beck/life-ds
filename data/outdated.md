# Outdated Data

Generated data is never repaired in place: when a generator or schema change leaves shipped datasets behind, the datasets are flagged here and regenerated from scratch with the current pipeline (see the no-repair rule in `AGENTS.md`). This file is maintained by hand — a session that notices outdated data adds an entry with the reason; whoever regenerates removes the entry once the fresh data is committed.

An entry names the affected data as precisely as known (a person id, a meta story id, or a corpus-wide condition), the generator change that outdated it, and what the reader loses until regeneration.

## Flagged for regeneration

- **All persons except `ada_lovelace`, `alan_turing`, `emmy_noether`, `frances_allen`, `franz_kafka`, `hedy_lamarr`, `max_planck`, `otto_wagner`** — events carry no `weight` (706 of 831 corpus events, as of 2026-08-31): they predate Phase 1 weighting. The interface still derives a fallback weight, but the deep-event selection is weaker than the weighed one, and `generate_event_backgrounds.py` refuses to fill reports for unweighted datasets. The same 44 German `life_events.json` copies also report stale against their English source (`python scripts/translate_all_persons.py --check --target-lang de`), so regeneration has to carry the translation with it. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_person.py`.
- **Most persons** — events carry no `background` report where the story would offer a depth layer (only the eight weighed persons above are filled): datasets predate the background-report step. Until regeneration, those stories offer no way down on the affected events; regeneration plus the pipeline's background step fills them.
- **All persons except `ada_lovelace`, `alan_turing`, `frances_allen`, and `hedy_lamarr`** — chapters carry no `illustration` (255 of 275 corpus chapters, as of 2026-08-31): they predate the chapter-art step, which `generate_person.py` runs as Step 5 unless `--skip-chapter-art` is given. `ChapterSlide.svelte` opens those chapters on the headline alone, without the abstract image the slide is composed around. Four of the weighed persons — `emmy_noether`, `franz_kafka`, `max_planck`, and `otto_wagner` — were regenerated for their weights without the art, so weighting alone does not imply the illustrations are there. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_person.py`, or fill the art alone with `scripts/generate_chapter_illustrations.py`.
- **`albert_einstein`, `antoni_gaud`, `cunigunde_of_luxembourg`, `e_t_a_hoffmann`, `frei_otto`, `grace_hopper`, `heinz_nixdorf`, `joseph_weizenbaum`, `steve_jobs`, `umberto_eco`, `zaha_hadid`** — the only `event_class` blocks are `birth` and `death`, the two the pipeline decides from the dates rather than from the model: every dataset here was last updated on or before 2025-12-20 and predates the `marriage_partnership`, `migration`, `invention`, and `publication` kinds. - **`albert_einstein`, `cunigunde_of_luxembourg`, `frei_otto`, `grace_hopper`, `heinz_nixdorf`, `joseph_weizenbaum`, `umberto_eco`, `zaha_hadid`** — `life_events.json` carries no `conclusion`, which the current chapter step writes as a required field. `ConclusionSlide.svelte` still prints its heading, its related persons, and its references, so the story closes on a headed box with no closing sentence.
