# Outdated Data

Generated data is never repaired in place: when a generator or schema change leaves shipped datasets behind, the datasets are flagged here and regenerated from scratch with the current pipeline (see the no-repair rule in `AGENTS.md`). This file is maintained by hand — a session that notices outdated data adds an entry with the reason; whoever regenerates removes the entry once the fresh data is committed.

An entry names the affected data as precisely as known (a person id, a meta story id, or a corpus-wide condition), the generator change that outdated it, and what the reader loses until regeneration.

Hidden persons and collections are not tracked here. An entry the registries mark `"hidden": true` (`python scripts/set_hidden.py --list`) is off the deployed site, so nothing a reader sees depends on its state, and it is regenerated with the current pipeline before it is shown again. Every list and count below therefore covers the visible corpus only: 33 persons and 4 collections as of 2026-09-05. A session that shows a hidden entry again audits it against the conditions below and adds it where it meets one.

## Flagged for regeneration

- **All persons except `ada_lovelace`, `alan_turing`, `albert_einstein`, `charles_babbage`, `frances_allen`, `grace_hopper`, `niels_bohr`** — the story predates the depth layer and the chapter art (as of 2026-09-05): events carry no `weight` (413 of 523 events), no event carries a `background` report, and chapters carry no `illustration` (140 of 175 chapters). Of the seven filled persons, `ada_lovelace` and `alan_turing` predate the shorter report: their reports were written to a 350-550 word target that is now 250-350, so they read longer than anything the pipeline writes today. Length is not a loss to the reader, so this alone is not a reason to regenerate them.

- **All persons except `niels_bohr` (ego network)** — the network predates one or more of the schema changes of 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-05. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_person_network.py`; the German derivations carry every field and token named here. Each condition names the datasets that still meet it (as of 2026-09-05):
  - **Every network except `niels_bohr`** carries `interaction_frequency` (`daily` through `rare`) on each of its connections (594 in all), a field the schema dropped on 2026-09-05: how often two people dealt with each other is rarely stated in the sources, so the value was the model's guess and could not be checked. The interface ignores the field, so the reader sees no difference on a chip.
  - **Every network except `charles_babbage`, `grace_hopper`, and `niels_bohr`** carries `influence_direction`, `start_year`, `end_year`, and `shared_activities` on each connection (568 in all) and was generated under a prompt that admitted one-sided ties: someone the subject only read or admired, or who only later drew on the subject's work. The schema now defines a connection as a mutual tie — two people who dealt with each other directly — and carries none of the four fields. The interface ignores the fields, but the story no longer filters an event's chips by the tie's years, so a connection whose recorded years excluded an event now appears on it, and the networks still hold ties the current prompt would leave out of the graph. The `shared_activities` handling in `translate_person.py` can go once no dataset carries the key.
  - **`albert_einstein`, `edsger_w_dijkstra`, `frances_allen`, `joseph_weizenbaum`, `zaha_hadid`** carry nodes that are not individuals: 20 connections typed `entity_kind: organization` or `group` ("Nazi regime" as Einstein's `political/persecutor`, "IBM" and "National Security Agency" as Allen's employer and client, six faculty and working-group collectives in Dijkstra's network), plus two untyped collectives in `joseph_weizenbaum` ("General Electric Computer Development Laboratory colleagues", "Students at MIT and other universities"). The generator's schema and prompt now admit only named human beings and ask for the person through whom such a tie ran; the interface still renders the old nodes as labeled chips, but a network reader sees an institution or a crowd where every other node is a person. The `entity_kind` and `qualifier` handling in `PersonChip.svelte`, `translate_person.py`, and the locales can go once no dataset carries them.
  - **`albert_einstein`, `joseph_weizenbaum`, `konrad_zuse`**, and the meta stories **`computing_pioneers`** and **`organic_shapes_in_architecture`**, carry an `intellectual/…` relationship type — one connection each in the ego networks, three links in all in the meta stories, derived from those networks — a category the vocabulary has retired into `academic`: the two split one circle in two (Einstein's network opens an "Intellectual (1)" circle holding Bohr alone beside "Academic (4)").   - **`ada_lovelace`, `charles_babbage`, `grace_hopper`, `steve_jobs`** — the first three label ordinary parents `family/biological_father`/`family/biological_mother` (issue #142): the closed vocabulary offered the token beside `father`/`mother` and asked for the most specific role, and every network generated under it took the qualified one. - **Corpus-wide: 44 annotations across 19 persons** (as of 2026-09-05) — the annotation explains a term that an earlier event of the same story already annotated or carried in its title: Babbage's "Lectures in Turin" and "Lovelace Publishes Engine Notes" both gloss the Analytical Engine two and three slides after "Designs Analytical Engine", Engelbart's and Bo Bardi's stories each carry six such repeats. Phase 2 researches each event in isolation, and `drop_repeated_annotations` in `scripts/events/normalize.py` now removes such repeats at the normalize step and on review; the shipped datasets predate the rule. The reader is offered a definition of what the previous slides were about. The German derivations carry the same annotations. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_person.py`, or run `scripts/review_person.py`, whose save path applies the rule.
- **Corpus-wide: 12 event images across 10 persons** (as of 2026-09-05: `abigail_adams`, `alan_turing`, `alvar_aalto`, `benjamin_franklin`, `george_washington`, `herman_hollerith`, `john_von_neumann`, `joseph_brant`, `konrad_zuse`, `zaha_hadid`) — the images are photographs of commemorations (a plaque, a grave, a statue, a memorial), which `score_commemoration` in `scripts/events/images/scoring.py` now penalizes so that a period photograph of the same subject outranks them; the shipped datasets were assembled while the penalty was unreachable and the score preferred the modern photograph. Regenerate with `scripts/generate_person.py`.
- **Corpus-wide: every prose field written before 2026-09-05** — descriptions, conclusions, background reports, annotation explanations, relationship descriptions, category summaries, and the meta stories' article prose and cards were generated before the writing phases shared `scripts/utils/prose_style.py`, and they carry the constructions it now rules out: 20 of the 31 background reports contrast a fact against an alternative nobody proposed ("rather than", "not X but Y"), 25 of them carry a colon introducing an explanation, about one description in twelve closes on a sentence saying what the event "marked" or "reflected", and the German derivations mirror all of it ("statt", "nicht ... sondern"). Nothing is wrong on the slide, so this alone is not a reason to regenerate a person; it resolves when a person or meta story is regenerated for any other reason, and `scripts/review_person.py` now rewrites such sentences under the same rules for a person regenerated in part.

- **Corpus-wide: 17 of 33 taglines** (as of 2026-09-05) — the tagline is built on a generic status noun (pioneer, architect, father, visionary, master, champion), mostly as "noun of ...": 'Pioneer of programmable computing', 'Architect of the Quantum Atom', 'Architect of relativity', 'Father of theoretical computing', 'Visionary of the Analytical Engine', six "Architect of ..." and eight "Pioneer of ..." in all. Phase 1 (`scripts/events/pipeline.py`) offered exactly these phrases as its examples, so the model reproduced the pattern, and on a card row the taglines stop telling the people apart. Nothing is wrong on the card, so this alone is not a reason to regenerate a person; the tagline is rewritten when a person is regenerated for any other reason with `scripts/generate_person.py`, and the German copy follows through `scripts/translate_person.py`.
